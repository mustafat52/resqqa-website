Technical FAQ

Questions engineers and procurement teams ask

Answers drawn from the manufacturer's catalogues. For a model-specific answer, message us.

How long do Amaron Quanta and Amaron Volt batteries last?

Per the manufacturer's datasheets, Amaron Quanta models from 26Ah to 200Ah have a design float life of up to 10 years, while the 7, 9, 12 and 18Ah models are rated for up to 6 years. Amaron Volt is rated at 20+ years at 27°C. Design-life figures assume rated conditions — higher operating temperatures and deep cycling reduce real-world life, which is why correct sizing and temperature control matter.

What charging voltage should I use?

Amaron Quanta 26–200Ah: float 13.5 V and cyclic 13.8 V per 12V battery at 27°C, with charge current limited to 25% of rated capacity (the 7–18Ah models use higher cyclic voltages — check the datasheet). Amaron Volt: float 2.23–2.25 volts per cell and boost 2.30–2.32 volts per cell, with maximum charging current of 0.3C (C being the 10-hour rated Ah).

Which international standards do the batteries meet?

Amaron Volt is certified to IS 15549:2005 and IEC 60896-21/22 and 61427, complies with IEEE 1188 & 1189 and Telcordia SR4228, and carries CE marking. Amaron Quanta conforms to JIS and is built in an ISO 9001-certified plant. Ask us for the specific certificate for your model.

Is a flame-retardant version available?

Yes, for Amaron Quanta 26Ah to 200Ah. The datasheets list flame-retardant versions in UL 94-V0 material, with model codes ending in 'Z' (for example 12ALZ100N). The 7–18Ah models use ABS cases.

What temperature range can the batteries run in?

Most Amaron Quanta models are rated for −20°C to +60°C (50–60°C for shorter periods); the 9Ah, 12Ah and 34Ah models are rated to +50°C. Amaron Volt is rated −40°C to +60°C. Capacity changes with temperature — the 100Ah datasheet shows 110% at 40°C, 100% at 27°C, 80% at 0°C and 60% at −15°C at the 20-hour rate.

What does HWS mean?

HWS is the High Wattage Series within Amaron Quanta — ratings of 388W, 440W, 540W and 730W (15-minute rating per cell at 1.67 ECV), designed for data centres and other float applications.

Can RESQ help me size the battery bank for my load?

Yes. Technical sizing is part of our service — send us your load (kW or kVA), required back-up time and any space limits, and we'll recommend the model and configuration, including racks and shelving where needed.

Are the batteries held in stock in Doha?

RESQ advertises ex-stock availability across the Amaron Quanta range. Stock levels change by model and capacity, so message us on WhatsApp with the exact rating you need and we'll confirm current availability and lead time.

Do you have datasheets for the other brands?

Datasheets for all 16 Quanta models, the Quanta catalogue and the Volt catalogue are available above. For Quanta Solar, Brute, Sleek, S-XEL, Genpro and Autopart Galaxy, ask us and we'll send the current manufacturer specifications.
